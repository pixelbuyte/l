The ones that are not negotiable

Everything else in the day bends. These are the ones the plan is built backwards from.

How the times are worked out

Blocks are placed as early as their order allows, then the planner runs the chain backwards from every hard deadline to find the latest each one could start. The difference is the slack shown against each row.

Where sunset comes from

Computed here, from your latitude and the date, using the NOAA solar equations — accurate to about a minute. Nothing is looked up and nothing is sent anywhere. Your location is asked for only if you press the button, and is kept in this browser.

What it does not do

It will not tell you that you are behind on a day you have not started, and it does not know about the things that actually take the afternoon. A missed deadline here is arithmetic, not a judgement.

Editing the routine

The day is one file — day/data/routine.js. Durations, order and deadlines all live there, and nothing else needs touching to change them.
