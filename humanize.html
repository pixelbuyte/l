The rewriter

Take the machine habits out of a draft.

Paste a piece of AI-generated text. Every habit from the pattern library gets found, explained and edited out, and the result is measured against the human corpus. It runs entirely in this page — nothing you paste leaves your browser.

What this does and does not do

It only ever deletes text, substitutes from a fixed list of alternatives, or splits a long sentence at a coordinating joint. There is no model behind it, so it cannot introduce a claim you did not write — the worst it can do is read flat.

That constraint has a cost, and the tool will tell you about it. The strongest signal separating machine prose from human prose is sentence rhythm, and rhythm is the one thing rules mostly cannot repair — you can only split a sentence that already contains two clauses. Where the rewrite comes out flat, the panel below says so and hands you the specific sentences to fix by hand.

It also makes no claim about detection software, and the measurements on this page identify a register, not an author. If you are somewhere that requires you to disclose AI assistance, editing the prose does not change what you owe them. This is a tool for making a draft read like you, which still means reading it afterwards.
